Overall purpose:  
It’s an SMS reminder and warm‑up sequence that runs automatically for people who book and confirm a “Relationship Reset Call” (or similar calls on several calendars). It sends multiple texts from the moment the call is booked up to 5 minutes before the appointment.

-----

### Triggers (how contacts enter the workflow)

There are 13 appointment-based triggers. They’re all variations of the same idea:

  - They fire when:
      
      - The event type is “normal”
      - The contact is either the main contact or a guest
      - The appointment status is confirmed
      - The appointment is in a specific calendar (each trigger points to a different calendar ID)

Names include:

  - “Copy of Relationship Reset Call”
  - Multiple “Relationship Reset Call” triggers (each tied to a different calendar)
  - “RR Call with Luann”
  - “Closer - Steph Waring Calendly”

Effect:  
Any time a call is confirmed on one of those calendars, the contact is enrolled into this SMS sequence.

-----

### Actions (what happens after the trigger)

Once a contact is enrolled, they go through this linear series of steps:

  - SMS 1:
      
      - Sent immediately after the appointment is confirmed.
      - Message:
          
          - Confirms the call time and date using appointment fields:  
            {{appointment.only\_start\_time}} and {{appointment.only\_start\_date}}
          - Asks them to reply “YES” to confirm.
          - Reminds them to watch the pre‑call information and follow instructions on the confirmation page.
  - Wait 30 seconds after SMS 1 (time-based wait):
      
      - Pauses for 30 seconds after SMS 1 is sent.
  - SMS 2:
      
      - Sent after that 30‑second wait.
      - Message:
          
          - Tells them you’ll send resources to their email.
          - Emphasizes reading those resources so they’re prepared.
          - Ends with a positive, excited tone.
  - 24 hours before the call (appointment-based wait):
      
      - Waits until 24 hours before the appointment start time.
      - If the appointment timing changes, this wait is tied to the appointment, so it adjusts relative to the new start time (within the platform’s normal appointment-wait behavior).
      - Condition is set to “skip” if the timing no longer makes sense (e.g., if it’s already inside that window when they enter).
  - SMS 3:
      
      - Sent 24 hours before the call.
      - Message:
          
          - Friendly reminder that you’re scheduled to chat tomorrow at {{appointment.only\_start\_time}}.
          - Keeps it short: “Talk soon”.
  - 6 hours before the call (appointment-based wait):
      
      - Waits until 6 hours before the appointment.
  - SMS 4:
      
      - Sent 6 hours before the call.
      - Message:
          
          - Sends a podcast link you and your husband recorded.
          - Explains it’s part 1 of 3 and asks for their thoughts.
          - This is a “warm‑up” / value‑add message to deepen engagement before the call.
  - 1 hour before the call (appointment-based wait):
      
      - Waits until 1 hour before the appointment.
  - SMS 5:
      
      - Sent 1 hour before the call.
      - Message:
          
          - “Your call with my team starts in 1 hour.”
          - Asks them to find a quiet spot.
          - Provides a reschedule link using {{custom\_values.precall\_booking\_link}} in case something came up.
  - 5 minutes before the call (appointment-based wait):
      
      - Waits until 5 minutes before the appointment.
  - SMS 6:
      
      - Sent 5 minutes before the call.
      - Message:
          
          - “5 minutes out\! We’ll see you soon {{[contact.name](http://contact.name/)}}.”
          - Final nudge to be ready right before the call.
