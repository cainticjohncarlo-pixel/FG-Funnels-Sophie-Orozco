Continuation Tracker System

How a client moves from the end of their program to the next one: five workflows, one pipeline board, and one tag when they buy.

Standard Operating Procedure · v2.0 · Sept 30, 2026
Start here

What this system is

When a client reaches the end of their program, this system offers them the next one and keeps score. It puts every graduate on one pipeline board, sends the offer at the right week, notices who clicks or replies, records who buys and for how much, and lists who declined. Nothing here needs a spreadsheet, and the only human step is adding one tag when a payment is confirmed.

Five pieces, listed in the order they happen. Each one names the workflow as it appears in GHL, what starts it, what it does step by step, and a flowchart.

The workflows

Five pieces, in the order they happen

All five are built in GHL. They switch on together once the Next Steps emails are finished, see the status at the bottom.

1
Workflow name in GHL

WF-CT1 Graduation Entry Built, not switched on

Puts every finishing client on the board one week before their program ends.

What starts it

Day 77 after program_start_date, for contacts carrying the tag existing client. Leads never enter.

What it does, step by step
  1. The day arrives for that client.
  2. Adds the tag graduated to the contact.
  3. Creates a card for the client in the Continuation Programs pipeline at the Graduated stage, status Open.
  4. Ends. Re-entry is off, so a client is only ever entered once.
What it means for the team

Luann can see everyone who is about to finish, in one column, before anyone has to remember.

2
Workflow name in GHL

WF- Week 4, Week 5 and Week 9 Next Steps (the outreach) Built, not switched on

The what-comes-next email is the offer. It lives in the Client Journey milestone workflows and reports back to this pipeline.

What starts it

The milestone day arrives: day 28 for RMM Plus, day 35 for the 6-session programs, day 63 for RFB 1 Year.

What it does, step by step
  1. Checks the program tag to decide men or women.
  2. Women receive Let's Keep Going Together. Men receive Step Into Your Power. Each email has five buttons, one per continuation program, and every button is a tracked link.
  3. Adds the tag outreach sent.
  4. Creates or updates the client's card at Outreach Sent.
  5. Waits 5 days.
  6. Checks for any enrolled tag or continuation-engaged. If found, ends, the client responded.
  7. If not, moves the card to Needs Follow-Up and creates the task Continuation follow-up for Luann, due in 3 days.
What it means for the team

Every graduate gets the offer at the right week, and anyone who goes quiet becomes a task for Luann five days later.

The outreach steps
3
Workflow name in GHL

WF-CT2 Continuation Engagement Built, not switched on

Notices interest. A click, a reply or a booking moves the card so Luann reaches out while it is warm.

What starts it

Any one of three things: the client clicks one of the seven Enroll links, replies to the email, or books an appointment.

What it does, step by step
  1. Checks the contact has the tag outreach sent. Anyone without it is not in the continuation flow, so their clicks, replies and bookings are ignored.
  2. Adds the tag continuation-engaged.
  3. Moves the card to Call Booked / Reply Received.
  4. Ends. Re-entry is on, so a second click or reply is fine, it just lands on the same stage.
What it means for the team

The Call Booked / Reply Received column is Luann's warm list. Nobody has to watch an inbox for clicks.

WF-CT2 Continuation Engagement
4
Workflow name in GHL

WF-CT3 Continuation Enrolled Built, not switched on

One tag records the sale, the program and the price on the pipeline.

What starts it

One of the seven enrolled-… tags is added to the contact. Seven triggers, one per tag.

What it does, step by step
  1. Reads which enrolled tag arrived.
  2. Moves the card to Enrolled.
  3. Sets the card's value to that program's price from the table.
  4. Ends.
What it means for the team

The revenue board fills itself. Whoever confirms a payment adds one tag and is done.

5
Workflow name in GHL

WF-CT4 Continuation Declined Built, not switched on

The sweep. Anyone who graduated and never enrolled is moved to Declined for a personal follow-up.

What starts it

Day 90 after program_start_date, for contacts carrying the tag graduated.

What it does, step by step
  1. Checks for any of the seven enrolled tags.
  2. If one is there, the client enrolled. Ends, nothing changes.
  3. If none, adds the tag declined-all and moves the card to Declined.
What it means for the team

The Declined column is the list to call. Nobody who bought ever lands there.
