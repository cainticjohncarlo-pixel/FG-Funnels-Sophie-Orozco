If this is your first time using Circle:
1. Click the course access link above.
2. Follow the prompts to create your Circle account.
3. Use the same email address you used when purchasing the program.
4. Create your password and complete your account setup.
5. Once your account is created, you’ll be taken inside Sophie Orozco Coaching’s Circle platform.
If you already have a Circle account with us, simply log in using your existing email address and password.
Step 3: Find Your Course
Once you’re logged into Circle:
1. Open the Sophie Orozco Coaching community.
2. Look for your Self-Paced Course in the spaces available to you.
3. Click on the course to open the curriculum.
4. Begin with the first lesson or module and work through the material in order.
5. Continue through each lesson at your own pace.
You will only see the course and materials included with your purchase. Private coaching, group coaching calls, and other community spaces are not included with Self-Paced access.
Step 4: Work Through the Program
There is no need to rush through the material.
We encourage you to:  
  :love_letter:  Watch each lesson in order
 :love_letter:  Complete the exercises and worksheets provided
  :love_letter:  Take time to reflect on what you’re learning
  :love_letter:  Apply the tools in your relationship as you go
  :love_letter:  Revisit previous lessons whenever you need a refresher
The goal isn’t simply to finish the course. The real value comes from consistently putting what you learn into practice.
Having Trouble Accessing Your Course?
First, make sure you are logging into Circle using the same email address associated with your purchase.
If you still cannot access your course, have trouble logging in, or experience another technical issue, please contact:
 :e-mail: 
 Hazel | Support Team
 support@sophieorozco.com
When contacting support, please include the name and email address used for your purchase so we can locate your account more quickly.
Office Hours & Team Support
Our team is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.
Please allow 24–72 business hours for a response.
One Final Reminder
Watching the lessons is only the beginning.
The transformation happens when you take what you learn and begin practicing it in your relationship.
Take your time. Stay consistent. Be willing to look at your own patterns and make the small shifts that can create a very different relationship experience.
We’re excited to have you here and can’t wait for you to get started.
With love,
 The RMM Team
