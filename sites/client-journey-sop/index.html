What this system is

From the moment a client buys until they finish their program, one system runs their journey: their first email, their start date, their coach handoff, Luann's check-ins, the client's milestone emails, the session count, and the alert before the last session. Everything is timed from each client's own start date, so nothing goes out in batches and nobody gets an email that does not match where they are.

Ten pieces, listed below in the order they happen. Each one names the workflow exactly as it appears in GHL, what starts it, what it does step by step, and a flowchart.

The whole journey
Three ideas that make it work
1One date

The start date is stamped once, at Closed Won. Every wait, alert and email counts from it. Nobody types dates.

2One tag decides the program

The program tag says what the client bought and whether they are a man or a woman. Onboarding, milestones and the continuation offer all read it.

3People do the human parts only

Assigning the coach, doing the check-in, having the conversation. The system does the timing, the records and the reminders.

The workflows

Ten pieces, in the order they happen

A green badge means it is running today. A grey badge means it is built in GHL and waiting to be switched on.

1
Workflow name in GHL

Onboarding Email- (one per program and gender) Live

Ten small workflows, one per program. The client's first email, with their Circle community invitation.

What starts it

The closer adds the program tag to the contact, for example client - rmm accelerator men. Each tag starts its own workflow. Nothing else starts it.

What it does, step by step
  1. Closer marks the sale Closed Won and adds the program tag, copied word for word from the Client Tagging SOP.
  2. The matching onboarding workflow starts within a minute.
  3. It sends the welcome email for that program, from hello@sophieorozco.com, with the Circle invitation link and the first steps.
  4. The workflow ends. It never sends anything else.
What it means for the team

The client hears from us minutes after buying. If the tag is wrong or missing, nothing sends and nobody is alerted, which is why the tagging SOP exists.

2
Workflow name in GHL

WF-Closed Won Client Setup Built, not switched on

Stamps the start date, sets the session count, and alerts Luann. Everything later is timed from this date.

What starts it

The opportunity card is moved to the Closed Won stage of the sales pipeline.

What it does, step by step
  1. Sets program_start_date on the contact to today's date.
  2. Sends Luann an internal email: New client, with the client's name and program.
  3. Reads the program and sets Sessions Purchased and Sessions Remaining: 6 for RMM, VIP, Couples and RFB, 12 for RFB 1 Year, 4 for RMM Plus. Programs without 1:1 sessions are left blank.
  4. Later, once the contract templates are loaded: sends the program's agreement for e-signature and files the signed copy on the contact.
What it means for the team

One date drives every email and alert that follows, so it is set by the system and never typed by hand.

WF-Closed Won Client Setup
3
Workflow name in GHL

WF- Coach Handoff Built, not switched on

The moment Luann assigns a coach, the coach gets the full client brief.

What starts it

The contact's Assigned User is set or changed. That is Luann picking the coach on the contact record.

What it does, step by step
  1. Luann opens the contact and chooses the coach in the Assigned To field.
  2. The workflow sends that coach an internal email: New client assigned to you, with name, email, phone, program, the intake answers, and where the signed agreement lives on the record.
  3. If the client is ever reassigned, the new coach receives the same brief.
What it means for the team

Coaches never chase Luann for details. One click on the record is the whole handoff.

4
Workflow name in GHL

WF-CS Milestone Tasks Live

Luann's own touchpoints: six emails and six tasks, timed from the day the client became a client.

What starts it

The tag existing client is added to the contact, which happens for every client at close.

What it does, step by step
  1. Day 7: sends the welcome email from luann@sophieorozco.com and creates the task 4 - Week 1 welcome for Luann.
  2. Day 21: sends the check-in email and creates 4 - Week 3 check-in.
  3. Day 42: sends the halfway email and creates 4 - Week 6 progress check-in.
  4. Day 63: sends the graduation call email with the booking link and creates 3 - Week 9 schedule graduation call.
  5. Day 85: sends the renewal conversation email and creates 1 - Renewal conversation.
  6. Day 90: creates 1 - Day 90 review renewal + remove Circle access. No client email, this one is internal.
What it means for the team

Luann works one task list, sorted by due date. The number at the front of each task puts renewals above routine check-ins. Every task opens the client's record.

WF-CS Milestone Tasks
5
Workflow name in GHL

WF-CS Accelerator Weekly Live

A weekly accountability task for Luann, Accelerator clients only, until the client completes.

What starts it

The tag client - accelerator men or client - accelerator women is added.

What it does, step by step
  1. Waits 7 days.
  2. Creates the task 4 - Verify accountability submission for Luann.
  3. Checks for the tag client - completed. If it is there, the loop ends.
  4. If not, goes back to the wait and repeats the following week.
What it means for the team

One task a week per Accelerator client, without anyone setting reminders. Adding client - completed at the end of the program stops it.

WF-CS Accelerator Weekly
6
Workflow name in GHL

Client Check-in Alerts Live

Date-based alerts to the client's CSM at each milestone, so nothing depends on remembering a calendar.

What starts it

A milestone day arrives, counted from program_start_date: day 21, 42, 63, 69 or 90. The contact must carry the tag cs alerts - date based.

What it does, step by step
  1. On each milestone day the workflow checks which milestone fired.
  2. Sends the milestone email to the address in the contact's csm_email field, and an in-app alert.
  3. On day 90 it also removes the cs alerts - date based tag, so the client drops out of the alert cycle.
What it means for the team

The CSM sees a client's milestone on the day it happens, even for clients enrolled months apart.

Client Check-in Alerts
7
Workflow name in GHL

WF- Week 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 12 (six milestone workflows) Built, not switched on

Emails to the client at the right week of their own program. Six workflows, one email each.

What starts it

The milestone day arrives, counted from program_start_date. Each workflow also checks the client's program, so only the right people get it.

What it does, step by step
  1. The day arrives for that client. A day that already passed when the client entered never fires, so nobody gets a week 3 email in week 10.
  2. The workflow checks the program. If the milestone does not apply, nothing sends.
  3. Check-in and completion emails send once and stop.
  4. The three Next Steps workflows do more: they split by gender, send the continuation offer with five program buttons, tag the contact outreach sent, put a card at Outreach Sent in the Continuation Programs pipeline, wait 5 days, and if the client has not clicked, replied or enrolled, move the card to Needs Follow-Up and create a task for Luann. That part is described in the Continuation Tracker SOP.
What it means for the team

Every client gets the same journey, on their own calendar, with the offer arriving exactly when their program is wrapping up.

The six milestone workflows
8
Workflow name in GHL

WF- Session Counter Built, not switched on

Keeps the session count on the client's record without a spreadsheet.

What starts it

An appointment on a coaching session calendar is marked Showed or No-Show. Sales and reset-call calendars are excluded.

What it does, step by step
  1. Adds 1 to Sessions Used.
  2. Subtracts 1 from Sessions Remaining.
  3. Ends. It runs again on every session, so re-entry is on.
What it means for the team

Any coach can open a record and see exactly where the client stands. No-shows count as used, as Chris decided.

WF- Session Counter
9
Workflow name in GHL

WF- Last Session Alert Built, not switched on

Warns the coach that the next session is the last, and tells the client what happens next.

What starts it

The Sessions Remaining field changes on the contact.

What it does, step by step
  1. Checks whether Sessions Remaining now equals 1.
  2. If yes, sends the assigned coach an internal notification: this client's final session is next.
  3. Sends the client a short note from Luann so questions about what comes next land in her inbox.
  4. If the number is anything other than 1, nothing happens.
What it means for the team

The continuation conversation starts before the last session, not after the client has gone quiet.

WF- Last Session Alert
10
Workflow name in GHL

Hand-off to the Continuation Tracker Built, not switched on

Where this system ends and the next one begins.

What starts it

Day 77 after program_start_date, handled by WF-CT1 Graduation Entry in the Continuation Tracker.

What it does, step by step
  1. The client is tagged graduated and a card is created at Graduated in the Continuation Programs pipeline.
  2. From there the Continuation Tracker records every click, reply, purchase or decline.
  3. See the Continuation Tracker SOP for that system.
What it means for the team

The two systems share one date, one pipeline and one set of tags, so a client never falls between them.

Your part

The only things a person does

WhenWhat you do
A sale closesMove the card to Closed Won, set Offer Discussed, add the program tag and existing client. Copy the tag from the Client Tagging SOP.
The new-client alert arrivesOpen the contact and assign the coach. That one click sends the coach their brief.
A task lands on your listOpen it, do the touchpoint, mark it complete. The task links to the client.
A client replies to any emailReply like normal email. The conversation is yours from there.
A session happensThe coach marks it Showed or No-Show on the calendar. That is the only bookkeeping.
The last-session alert arrivesHave the continuation conversation when the moment is right.
An Accelerator client finishesAdd client - completed. The weekly task stops.
You want an email's wording changedSend John the new text. It is swapped in minutes.
Never add a client into a workflow by hand. The emails are timed from dates. A manual add has no dates and can fire every email at once. Never edit a workflow yourself, one change can affect every enrolled client. Never re-add a tag a client already had, that restarts automations. Message John instead, changes take minutes.
