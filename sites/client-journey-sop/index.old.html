The Big Picture

The system runs each client's journey by itself

From the moment a client signs up until they finish their program, everything is timed to each client's own start date — records, check-in emails, session counts, and your alerts. Nothing goes out in batches, and no client ever gets an email that doesn't match where they are in their program.

The Five Automations

What each one does, in plain language

1When a Client Signs Up

System name: WF-K1 Closed Won Client Setup
What starts it

A sale is marked as won.

What it does, all by itself
  • Records that day as the client's official program start date — every future email is timed from it
  • Reads which program they bought and sets their session count: 4, 6, or 12
  • Sends you an alert for every new client — coaching and non-coaching — so you track everyone from day one
  • (Once contracts are loaded) sends their program's agreement for e-signature and files the signed copy on their profile
Flow
Sale marked won
↓
Start date recorded
↓
Session count set (4 / 6 / 12)
↓
Alert lands in your inbox
↓
Contract sent for e-signature

Built-in safety
If a client is already past a milestone when they enter the system, that email is skipped rather than sent late. Nobody ever gets a “week 3” email in week 10.

3The Session Counter

System name: WF-K3 Session Counter
What starts it

A session on a coach's calendar is marked as attended — or as a no-show (no-shows count as used, as you decided).

What it does
  • Adds one to Sessions Used and subtracts one from Sessions Remaining on the client's profile
  • Any coach can glance at a record and see exactly where the client stands
  • Nobody keeps a spreadsheet
Flow
Session marked attended or no-show
↓
Sessions Used +1
↓
Sessions Remaining −1
↓
Count visible on the client's profile

4The Last-Session Alert

System name: WF-K4 Last Session Alert
What starts it

A client's Sessions Remaining reaches 1.

What it does
  • Alerts their coach that the next session is the final one
  • Sends the client a heads-up note from you — so their questions about what happens next come straight to your inbox
Flow
Sessions Remaining hits 1
↓
Coach alerted: “final session next”
Client notified — from you
↓
You have the continuation conversation

5The Coach Handoff

System name: WF-K5 Coach Handoff
What starts it

You assign a client to a coach in the system — one click.

What it does
  • Instantly sends that coach everything they need: the client's name, program, intake summary, and where to find their signed agreement
  • If you ever reassign a client, the new coach gets the same brief automatically
Flow
You assign the coach
↓
Coach receives the full brief
↓
Coach sees only their own clients in the system

Important

Please never do these — ask John instead

🚫
Never add a contact into a workflow manually

The emails are timed to each client's dates. A manual add has no dates attached and fires every email at once.

🚫
Never edit the workflows themselves

Even small changes can affect every enrolled client at once.

🚫
Never re-add a tag a client already had

That can restart automations and duplicate emails to the client.

Anything you want changed or fixed: message John. Changes take minutes and stay safe.
