Every message I draft for the user to post to a client team (Slack replies, updates, fixes, EODs) must sound like a person wrote it: concise, direct, as short as possible, straight to the point. No preamble, no restating the problem when the reader already knows it, no "here is the fix" framing, no closing pleasantries.

**Why:** Client Chris Orozco (Sophie Orozco Coaching) called an earlier reply "AI-sounding" and said it "talks in circles". The user reminded me again on 24 Sep 2026 after I drafted a multi-paragraph fix message that should have been four lines.

**How to apply:** Lead with the action. Numbered steps only when there are steps. Cut every sentence that does not tell the reader something they need. Related: [[feedback-client-email-first-person]], [[feedback-outreach-copy-style]].
