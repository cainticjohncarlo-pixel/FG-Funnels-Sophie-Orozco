In any client-facing email, update, or proposal I draft for John Carlo, use first person
singular: "I", "my", "me". Never "we", "our", "us".

**Why:** he is a solo builder. He does the work himself, so "we" implies a team that does
not exist and misrepresents the engagement.

**How to apply:** sweep every draft before handing it over. The usual offenders are
"we have created", "our suggestion is", "everything we need", "one thing we noticed",
and "once we have X we can do Y". Rewrite all of them to the singular.

Applies to client comms specifically. Internal build docs and checklists written for him
or another builder to execute can stay neutral or use "you".
